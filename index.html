About The Event

Presented by IEEE SB CE Kallooppara

Skill Bridge 2.0 is the flagship technical event of IEEE Student Branch College of Engineering Kallooppara. It brings together the brightest minds for a 3-day extravaganza of workshops, tech talks, and networking opportunities. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry demands.
